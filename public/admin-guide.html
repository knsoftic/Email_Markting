Andar jana

Aam login screen se hi sign in karein. Agar aap ka account super admin hai to sidebar mein Super Admin ka badge dikhega aur panel /admin par khulta hai.

Sab se pehle

Server par pehli baar aane ke baad super admin ka password foran badlein. Seeder use .env ki KNS_ADMIN_PASSWORD se leta hai, aur woh placeholder public repository mein likha hua hai.

Server par: php artisan kn:admin-password — typing chhupi rehti hai, aur wahi password policy lagti hai jo baqi sab par hai.

Super admin ka koi tenant nahi hota. Isi liye woh har account ka data dekh sakta hai — aur isi liye ye account sab se ehtiyat wala hai.

Overview

Sirf parhne ke liye

Platform ka pehla screen. Kul accounts, users, contacts, campaigns aur bheje gaye email.

Ghalat parhne ki jagah

Yahan ke saare numbers install ke din se ab tak ke hain — "is mahine" ke nahi. Sirf neeche ke do charts 14 din ke hain.

Aur ye ginti har jagah ek jaisi nahi: kuch cards delete ki hui rows bhi ginte hain, kuch nahi. Kisi customer ka asli istemal dekhna ho to us ke apne account page par jayein — wahan wohi ginti hai jis par plan ki had lagti hai.

Accounts

Dekhna aur dhoondna

Har account ek alag tenant hai — apna data, apne users, apni limits. List mein naam, owner, plan, users, contacts, campaigns aur status dikhta hai. Upar se naam ya owner ke email se dhoond sakte hain, aur status ya plan se filter kar sakte hain.

Manage par click kar ke us account ka poora page khulta hai: paanch cards, account ki tafseel, subscription, aur per-account limit overrides.

Note

Yahan se naya account nahi banta. Account ya to customer khud register kar ke banata hai, ya aap Users → New user se banate hain — wahan naya user banane par uska account bhi ban jata hai.

Account suspend karna

Foran asar — magar wapas ho sakta hai

Suspend karne par us account ke saare users agli hi request par logout ho jate hain aur dobara login nahi kar sakte. Data kahin nahi jata.

Aur is ke saath ye sab bhi ruk jata hai:

  • Chalta hua campaign wahin ruk jata hai — fail nahi hota, ruk jata hai.
  • Scheduled campaigns scheduled hi rehte hain, jate nahi.
  • Automations aage nahi barhtin.
  • Mailbox sync band ho jati hai.

Activate karte hi ye sab wahin se chal parta hai jahan ruka tha. Sirf woh log jo logout ho gaye thay, unhein dobara login karna parhta hai.

Ehtiyat

"Pending" bhi bilkul suspended jaisa hai. Account tab tak kaam nahi karta jab tak status theek Active na ho. Naya account "pending" par ho to customer ko lagta hai kuch kharab hai — status dekh lein.

Plan badalna aur barhana

Foran asar

Account ke page par dayen taraf Subscription ka card hai. Wahan se plan badlein, status (trial / active / expired / cancelled) chunein, expiry date dein, ya Extend by (days) mein dinon ka number likh kar date aage barha dein.

  1. Naya plan chunein.
  2. Status chunein — paisay mil gaye to Active.
  3. Expiry date dein, ya dinon mein extend karein.
  4. Internal note mein likh dein ke kyun — ye sirf aap dekhte hain.
  5. Update subscription.

Nayi limits foran lag jati hain — customer ko dobara login karne ki zaroorat nahi.

Note

Agar naye plan ki had customer ke maujooda istemal se kam hai, to uska data delete nahi hota. Bas woh us had se aage nahi barh sakta jab tak neeche na aa jaye.

Aur agar us par per-account overrides lagay huay thay, to woh saath chale jate hain — screen aap ko bata deti hai ke kitne rakhe gaye.

Limit overrides — ek customer ke liye alag had

Foran asar

Kabhi kisi ek customer ko plan se zyada dena hota hai — bina naya plan banaye. Account ke page par Per-account limit overrides isi ke liye hai.

Har khaana khaali chhorna ka matlab: "plan ka apna number use karo". Number likhna: "is customer ke liye yehi number".

Yaad rakhein

Khaali aur 0 ek cheez nahi hain. Khaali = plan ka number. 0 = us feature ko bilkul band kar do. Aur kuch jagah khaali ka matlab "bay-hisab" bhi ho sakta hai — number likhne se pehle plan ki value dekh lein.

Plans

Har us account par foran asar jo is plan par hai

Plan tay karta hai ke customer kya kar sakta hai: kitne contacts, kitne email, kitne mailboxes, aur kaun se features (automations, segments, A/B testing, apna SMTP).

Sab se ahem

Plan edit karte hi woh tabdeeli us par baithe har account par foran lag jati hai. Screen ye nahi batati ke kitne accounts is plan par hain — pehle Plans list par "Subscribers" column dekh lein.

Misal: max_emails_per_month ko us number se neeche karna jo customer is mahine pehle hi bhej chuka hai, uske chalte huay campaign ko wahin rok dega.

Khaali khaana = bay-hisab

Naya plan banate waqt har limit ka khaana khaali hota hai. Agar aap Limits card ko haath na lagayein, to woh plan har cheez mein bay-hisab ban jayega — sifar wala nahi. Har khaane mein number likhein.

Ehtiyat

allow_custom_smtp aur allow_admin_smtp dono band kar dene se us plan ke customers ke paas bhejne ka koi raasta nahi bachta.

Plan delete karna soft delete hai: woh list se hat jata hai (yani naye accounts ko nahi diya ja sakta), magar jo accounts pehle se us par hain unki limits waise hi chalti rehti hain jab tak aap unhein kisi aur plan par na le jayein.

Users

Kisi ka access band ya khol sakta hai

Platform ke saare log — har account ke, aur super admins bhi. Naam ya email se dhoondein, account ya status se filter karein.

Naya user banana

New user se banaya gaya user apne naye account mein jata hai — kisi maujooda account mein nahi. Yani ye "customer banana" hai.

  1. Naam, email aur password dein — password 10 harf ka, harf aur number dono ke saath.
  2. Company ka naam dein — yehi account ka naam banega.
  3. Plan chunein.
  4. Mark email address as verified tick kar dein, warna woh pehle login par verification screen par atkega.
Note

Password unhein email nahi hota — aap ko khud bhejna parhega. Aur email address poore platform mein ek hi baar istemal ho sakta hai.

Deactivate aur Delete ka farq

Suspend: login band, data mojood, kabhi bhi wapas kholein. Delete: user chhup jata hai magar uski activity ka record rehta hai. Apna account na aap suspend kar sakte hain na delete — panel mana kar deta hai.

"Log in as" — customer ban kar dekhna

Aap us customer ka sab kuch dekh aur kar sakte hain

Customer kahe "mujhe ye screen aisi dikh rahi hai" — to uske user par jayein aur Log in as dabayein. Aap bilkul wahi dekhenge jo woh dekhta hai.

Wapas aane ke liye upar Stop impersonating.

Jaan lein

Is dauran aap us customer ke contacts, campaigns aur inbox sab parh sakte hain — aur badal bhi sakte hain. App ke liye us waqt aap wohi customer hain.

Aur isi wajah se: jo bhi aap us dauran karenge, woh us customer ke naam se uski apni activity log mein likha jayega — aap ke naam se nahi. Shuru aur khatam hone ka record admin activity log mein rehta hai, magar beech ka kaam customer ke khatay mein. Is liye sirf dekhne ke liye istemal karein, kaam karne ke liye nahi.

Note

Doosre super admin ko impersonate nahi kiya ja sakta. Suspended user ya suspended account ko bhi nahi.

Roles & Permissions

Har us shakhs par asar jo ye role rakhta hai

Teen role pehle se bane huay hain: Super Admin, Account Owner aur Staff. Pehle do ke paas hamesha sab kuch hota hai aur unhein badla nahi ja sakta — panel ki matrix un par band rehti hai.

Staff badla ja sakta hai, aur yahan badalna har account ke staff par lagta hai. Woh shuru mein sirf paanch cheezein dekh sakta hai: contacts, campaigns, templates, inbox aur analytics.

Ehtiyat

Form mein koi bhi box tick kiye baghair save karna jaiz hai — aur us role se saari permissions hat jati hain. Save se pehle matrix dekh lein.

Admin SMTP — mushtarka bhejne ka raasta

Bhejne ka raasta — ghalat setting sab ka bhejna rok deti hai

Kuch customers apna SMTP nahi lagate — jaise Starter plan wale, jinhein ijazat hi nahi. Un ka email aap ke diye hue SMTP account se jata hai. Wohi yahan banate hain.

  1. Admin SMTP → Add.
  2. Host, port, username aur password bharein.
  3. Ghanta, din aur mahine ki had likhein — provider ki apni limit se neeche.
  4. Test dabayein — ye asli connection banata hai.
  5. Save ke baad assignment zaroor karein.
Sab se aam ghalti

Naya SMTP account bana kar assignment na karna. Woh live to hota hai magar us tak kisi ka raasta nahi hota — list par "Reaching nobody" likha aata hai. Assignment se hi tay hota hai ke ye kis plan ya kis account ke liye hai.

Aur assignment save karte waqt: woh poori list badal deta hai, us mein jorhta nahi. Save se pehle dekh lein ke jo pehle se chuna hua tha woh abhi bhi chuna hua hai.

Cooldown

Lagatar nakami par account khud thandey mein chala jata hai aur kuch der bhejta nahi. Password theek karne se woh khud nahi khulta — Clear cooldown dabayein ya Test chalayein.

Had poori hone par bhejna rukta hai, fail nahi hota. Agli window khulte hi khud chal parta hai.

Branding

Har customer ki screen par dikhta hai

Product ka naam, logo, favicon aur rang. Ye poore platform ke liye ek hi hai — har customer ko yehi dikhta hai. Har account ka alag logo nahi ho sakta.

Note

Branding customers ke bheje huay emails par nahi jati. Woh email customer ke apne blocks se bante hain aur us mein us ki apni company ka naam aata hai. Branding sirf app ki screens par hai.

Ehtiyat

Naya logo upload karte hi purana file mit jati hai — koi confirmation nahi, aur wapas nahi milti. Apni asli file kahin aur sambhal kar rakhein.

PNG, JPG ya WebP chalti hain. SVG jaan boojh kar nahi: SVG mein script ho sakti hai aur ye file har logged-in shakhs ko serve hoti hai.

System settings

Naye aur mojooda users par asar

Naye sign-ups khule hain ya band, naya account kis plan par aata hai, aur email verification zaroori hai ya nahi.

Ehtiyat

Email verification on karna woh ek setting hai jo pehle se logged-in logon ko beech kaam mein rok deti hai — agle page par unhein verification screen mil jayegi.

Aur registration band karne se uske links chhupte nahi. Login page par "Create one" abhi bhi dikhega; dabane par mana kar dega.

Neeche Environment values ka card sirf dikhane ke liye hai — woh .env se aata hai aur yahan se badla nahi ja sakta.

Payment settings

Sirf aap ke apne record ke liye
Saaf baat

Ye screen paisay nahi leti. Is product mein koi payment gateway nahi hai, aur ye tafseelat customers ko kahin dikhayi nahi jatin. Ye sirf aap ke apne record ke liye hain, taake aap ki shartein ek jagah likhi rahein.

Paisay product se bahar aate hain — bank transfer, cash, jo bhi aap ka tareeqa ho. Customer ka access asal mein Accounts par plan assign ya extend karne se badalta hai, yahan kuch bharne se nahi.

System health

Dekhna mehfooz — magar ek button ehtiyat maangta hai

Database, cache, storage aur queue theek hain ya nahi. Kuch bhi ajeeb lage to pehle yahan dekhein.

Badge se zyada tafseel parhein

Storage: Attention aksar sirf ye kehta hai ke storage:link nahi chala — disk bilkul theek ho sakti hai. Neeche wali line asal baat batati hai.

IMAP client: Healthy ka matlab sirf itna hai ke library lagi hui hai. Ye kisi customer ke mailbox se connection ki zamanat nahi.

Clear caches

Ye kuch poochhta nahi. Production par woh cache mit jati hai jo deploy ke waqt banti hai, aur panel mein use dobara banane ka koi button nahi — server par bash deploy.sh chalana parhta hai. Login throttling bhi reset ho jati hai.

Queue

"Clear failed jobs" wapas nahi hota

Campaign bhejna, imports, mailbox sync aur automations — sab queue se chalte hain. Ye screen batati hai ke kitna kaam intezaar mein hai aur kitna nakaam hua.

Worker chal raha hai ya nahi

"In progress: 0" ka matlab worker band hona nahi hai — khali queue par worker kuch uthata hi nahi. Asal ishara amber patti hai, jo tab aati hai jab kaam intezaar mein ho magar koi utha na raha ho. Wohi worker band hone ki nishani hai.

Clear failed jobs

Ye saare nakaam jobs hamesha ke liye mita deta hai — sirf screen par dikhne wale 25 nahi. Us ke baad na unhein dobara chalaya ja sakta hai na wajah dekhi ja sakti hai. Pehle list parhein, phir mitayein.

Retry all failed

Ye bina poochhe chal parta hai, aur poore platform ke nakaam jobs par lagta hai. Agar asal wajah abhi theek nahi hui (galat SMTP password, band mailbox), to woh sab dobara nakaam ho kar wapas aa jayenge. Pehle wajah theek karein, phir retry.

Activity log

Sirf parhne ke liye — mitaya nahi ja sakta

Poore platform mein kis ne kya kiya. Account, event ya tareekh se filter karein.

Note

Ye log na mitaya ja sakta hai na export — panel se aisa koi raasta nahi, aur yehi baat use qabil-e-etibaar banati hai. Rows sirf tab jati hain jab poora account database se hi mita diya jaye.

Rozana kaam

KaamKahanKya dhyan rakhein
Naya customer banana Users → New user Company ka naam account banata hai. Password khud bhejna parhega.
Paisay aa gaye — plan chalu karo Accounts → Manage → Subscription Status "Active", expiry date, aur note likh dein.
Paisay nahi aaye Accounts → Suspend Bhejna bhi ruk jata hai. Wapas activate karne par wahin se chalta hai.
Ek customer ko thori zyada had Account page → Limit overrides Khaali = plan ka number. 0 = band.
Customer kehta hai screen kharab hai Users → Log in as Sirf dekhein. Jo karenge woh unke naam likha jayega.
"Email nahi ja rahe" Queue → phir Admin SMTP Amber patti = worker band. Warna SMTP cooldown ya limit dekhein.
Naya shared SMTP Admin SMTP → Add Test karein, phir assignment zaroor — warna kisi tak nahi pohanchta.
Qeematein badalni hain Plans Pehle "Subscribers" column dekhein — tabdeeli sab par foran lagti hai.
